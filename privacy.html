the boring bit

the privacy policy for Stuff: Grocery List, said plainly. last changed 4 november 2026.

what we collect

nothing. there are no stuff servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. we don't have an account system, so we don't know who you are.

what stays on your phone

your list, pantry, and what you bought when. that's how the "forgot these?" guesses work. they're worked out on your phone, never in the cloud.

your icloud

your shika and the things you got with it are kept in your own icloud (apple's key-value storage), so they come back on a new phone. that space belongs to your apple id. we can't see it.

what apple sees

payments, if you buy something. apple handles the money. we get a receipt, not your card or your name.

the mic

only when you tap it. what you say is turned into list stuff on this phone using apple's on-device speech recognition. the audio is never sent anywhere and never saved.

notifications

if you say yes to pokes, the app schedules at most one local reminder a day, on your phone. nothing is sent from a server.

age

if your phone shares an age range, the app uses it once to decide whether to hide the shop for kids under 13. the answer isn't stored or sent anywhere.

backups

only if you tap "save a copy". the file is yours, and it goes wherever you send it.

wipe it all

settings → forget my history. or delete the app, which removes everything on the phone.

kids

stuff doesn't collect anything from anyone, kids included.

changes

if this ever changes, this page and the in-app privacy page will change together, with a new date at the top.

questions

see the support page.

stuff is made by one person. "data not collected" is the whole point.
