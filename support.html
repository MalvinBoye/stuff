need a hand?

help for Stufg: Grocery List. a cute grocery list that lives on your phone.

write to me

email stufg@nuffg.com. i read everything and reply within a day.

i bought something and it's gone

open stufg → settings → your purchases → restore purchases. it comes back through your apple id. no account needed.

my shika is gone on a new phone

shika lives in your own icloud. check you're signed into the same apple id, with icloud turned on for stufg in your phone's settings.

i want a refund

apple handles refunds. go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

the mic doesn't work

stufg only listens on your phone, so it needs on-device speech for your language. if your phone doesn't have it yet, typing or pasting works just as well.

the guesses are wrong

tap ✕ on a guess to hide it for a week. they get better after a few finished shops. to start over, settings → forget my history.

my kid keeps opening the shop

settings → hide the shop. bringing it back needs face id or your passcode.
